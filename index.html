⚙️ Computer Vision Segmentation Pipeline

How the system processes raw user strokes from the screen into isolated, normalized character tensors ready for CNN inference.

Step 1

Canvas Ingestion

Captures continuous mouse/stylus strokes using Bézier curve interpolation to eliminate jagged edge artifacts.

Step 2

Otsu Binarization

Polarity inversion ensures dark strokes on white paper map strictly to white glyph pixels on black background.

Step 3

Contour Segmentation

Connected-component analysis isolates individual characters, filters noise, and groups dots on 'i' and 'j'.

Step 4

Center-of-Mass Centering

Scales each glyph to a 20×20 bounding box and centers its center of mass into a 28×28 standard tensor.

Step 5

DeepCNN Inference

Standardized tensor passes through dual Conv2D+BN blocks and dense layers to yield 62-class probabilities.
